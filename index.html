Questions

Is there a monthly fee?

No. Your card and profile page are included in the one-off price and keep working for good. Pro is an optional extra at £4 a month or £39 a year. If you cancel it, your card keeps working with your basic profile.

Do people need an app?

No. Most modern iPhones and Android phones read NFC cards straight away. Phones without NFC can scan the QR code with the camera.

What if my phone number or job changes?

Log in and update your details. The change goes live straight away, so your cards keep working and never need reprinting.

Can I order cards for my whole team?

Yes. Each person gets their own card and profile page. Get in touch with the number of cards you need and we'll send a quote.

Does the metal card really work?

Yes. Platinum cards are built so the chip can still be read through the metal, and we test them on iPhone and Android before sending.
